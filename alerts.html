Congress Stock Trades

U.S. members of Congress must publicly disclose stock trades under the STOCK Act, usually within 45 days. I can't run a 24/7 background watcher, but these are the best free, constantly-updating live trackers — open them anytime for the newest disclosed buys.

🏛️
Capitol Trades
Free, well-organized feed of every disclosed trade by sitting members of Congress — filterable by politician, party, chamber, and ticker.
Open Live Feed → 📊
Quiver Quantitative
Free congressional trading tracker with historical data, ticker-level filters, and the most-traded names by Congress this year.
Open Live Feed → 🐳
Unusual Whales — Politics
Tracks congressional trades plus the NANC/GOP ETFs that mirror Democrat/Republican holdings in aggregate.
Open Live Feed →
Why no live alert feed here: These sites render their trade tables with JavaScript and block automated scraping, so I can't pull a reliable structured feed into this page or push you a real-time notification the instant a politician files a trade. The fastest way to get close to "alert me immediately" is to set up free email/push alerts directly on Capitol Trades or Quiver Quant for the specific politicians or tickers you care about — both support per-ticker watchlists.
