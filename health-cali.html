Foods to Eat and Avoid

Eat These Every Week

  • Leafy greens (spinach, kale, arugula) — vitamins, minerals, fiber, and anti-inflammatory compounds
  • Fatty fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel) — omega-3s for brain, joints, and inflammation 2–3× per week
  • Legumes (lentils, black beans, chickpeas) — fiber + protein + slow carbs for sustained energy
  • Berries (blueberries, raspberries, blackberries) — highest antioxidant density of any fruit per calorie
  • Eggs — the most complete food on earth. Eat them whole. The yolk is where the nutrients live.
  • Sweet potatoes — Vitamin A, potassium, fiber, and complex carbs that don't spike blood sugar sharply
  • Fermented foods (yogurt, kimchi, sauerkraut) — gut health directly affects immunity and mood
  • Nuts and seeds (walnuts, flax, chia) — healthy fats, zinc, magnesium, anti-inflammatory

Avoid or Minimize

  • Seed oils (canola, soybean, vegetable oil) — highly processed and pro-inflammatory. Use olive oil or butter.
  • Sugary drinks (sodas, juices, sports drinks) — liquid calories with zero nutritional value
  • Ultra-processed snacks (chips, crackers, cookies) — engineered to be addictive, low in nutrients
  • White bread and refined carbs — stripped of fiber, spike blood sugar rapidly
  • Alcohol — disrupts sleep quality, suppresses fat burning for up to 24 hours
  • Fast food — calorie-dense, nutrient-poor, loaded with sodium and artificial ingredients
  • Artificial sweeteners — linked to gut microbiome disruption and increased sugar cravings

The Calisthenics Mindset

Calisthenics isn't just exercise — it's mastery of your own body. The goal isn't to look a certain way. The goal is to move powerfully, age well, and maintain physical capability for life. A 60-year-old who can do 20 pull-ups, a clean pistol squat, and a 2-mile run is wealthier in health than most 25-year-olds in a gym. Invest in your movement. Your future self will thank you. Philippians 4:13 — you can do all things through Christ who strengthens you. That includes one more rep.
