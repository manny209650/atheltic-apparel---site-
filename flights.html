How To Actually Find a Deal

🛫

Check the Small Airports First

OAK and SMF are often $30–$100 cheaper than SFO on the same route because of lower airport fees and more budget carriers. SCK and MCE run limited leisure/EAS routes, but are worth a glance when they fly your dates.

📅

Stay Flexible ± 3 Days

Shifting your departure by a day or two — especially off Fridays and Sundays — routinely drops fares 15–30%. Use Google Flights' built-in price calendar after you search to spot the cheap day.

🔔

Set a Price Tracker

Once you land on a Google Flights result page, hit "Track prices" — you'll get an email the moment fares on that exact route drop, no need to keep checking manually.

🕵️

Search Incognito

Repeated searches can nudge dynamic pricing up on some sites. Comparing in a private/incognito window keeps your search history from skewing what you see.

🧳

Watch for Basic Economy Traps

The cheapest fare often blocks carry-ons or seat selection. Add a bag before checkout and compare the real total — Southwest (heavy at OAK/SMF) still includes two free checked bags.

⏱️

Book the Sweet Spot

Domestic fares from NorCal are typically cheapest 3–8 weeks out for leisure routes, and 1–3 months out for holidays and Hawaii/Mexico runs. Booking same-week is almost always the most expensive option.
