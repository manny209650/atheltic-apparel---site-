China Import Assumptions

These defaults are placeholders — replace them with real supplier quotes and freight rates. Guatemala uses the Central American tariff (SAC) and has no free-trade agreement with China, so standard duty (DAI) rates apply, plus 12% IVA on the value including freight and duty. Solar equipment is often 0% duty; glass and stone usually carry duty. Have a licensed agente aduanero confirm the tariff codes before you order. Sea freight Shanghai → Puerto Quetzal is typically 30–45 days. Order tempered or laminated safety glass sized to final measurements taken on site, 5–10% spare marble, and pay for a third-party inspection before the container is loaded. Local prices are supplied-and-installed; imported items add the installation rates shown.
